# Use case and sources

## Use case

- **Direction:** launch of AI Workspace Layer.
- **Owner:** Product Lead.
- **Recurring result:** Weekly Launch Update.
- **Recipient:** CEO and launch team.
- **Decision supported:** understand status, top risks, blockers, and decisions requiring leadership.
- **Trigger:** weekly launch sync.
- **Primary risk:** confusing a customer signal with a commitment, using stale context, or hiding missing evidence.

## Boundary

These are synthetic repository sources. Their existence does not demonstrate access to a real company system or permission to send real data to an external model.
