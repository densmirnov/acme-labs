# Quality Rubric

Use this rubric to evaluate a weekly launch update.

## Scoring

Score each criterion from 0 to 2.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Status clarity | status unclear or buried | status exists but needs interpretation | status is clear in the first section |
| Risk quality | risks missing or vague | risks present but not prioritized | top risks explicit and concrete |
| Decision usefulness | no leadership decisions | decisions implied but not actionable | decisions needed are explicit |
| Factual discipline | unsupported or invented facts | facts and assumptions mixed | facts, assumptions, and gaps separated |
| Operational usability | major rewrite needed | moderate editing needed | minimal editing needed |

## Pass Threshold

A usable update should score at least 7/10 and must not score 0 on factual discipline.
