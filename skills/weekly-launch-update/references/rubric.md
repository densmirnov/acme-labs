# Quality Rubric

Before scoring, check `../../../evaluations/weekly-launch-update/hard-gates.md`. A failed gate cannot be compensated by points.

Score each criterion from 0 to 2.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Factual accuracy | unsupported facts or hidden conflict | mostly correct, some claims need verification | all claims grounded and conflicts visible |
| Risks and decisions | missing | present but not actionable | prioritized risks and explicit leadership decisions |
| Gaps and uncertainty | hidden | partially stated | all material gaps and assumptions visible |
| Operational usability | full rewrite needed | noticeable editing needed | limited owner review |

Record human edit time separately. A usable result normally scores 7–8 and passes every applicable hard gate.
